# Dossier template

One dossier per skill, at `research/<skill-name>/dossier.md`. No SKILL.md is
written until sections 1 to 4 are filled and the baseline in section 5 is run.

## 1. Who and when
Who does this work, and the exact moment the skill should load.

## 2. Documented failures
10 to 20 failure cases. Each one: what goes wrong, a source that was opened
and read, and whether the source is verified or still to check.

## 3. Ground truth
Public datasets, baselines and tools the pitfall tasks are graded against.

## 4. Neighbours
Existing skills and tools. For each: route to it, or explain what this skill adds.

## 5. Baseline
Pass rate of the base model on the pitfall tasks with no skill loaded.
Build only where it fails.

## 6. Reviewers
Two or three candidate named reviewers and why each.

## 7. Regional variants
What changes by country, language, hardware vendor or regulator.

## 8. Limits
What the skill must refuse or caveat (research-only, not clinical, not certification).
